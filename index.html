Impact Evaluation Readiness Checklist

This checklist helps you assess whether your program is ready for a full-scale impact evaluation.

How to use it:

  • Read each item carefully across all three sections
  • Check the box only if you can confidently say your program meets the criterion
  • When finished, click "See my results" for your readiness level and recommended next steps

For a more accurate assessment, consider completing this checklist as a team discussion. Be honest in your evaluation: checking fewer boxes now helps you better prepare for impact evaluation. Revisit this checklist periodically as your program develops.
